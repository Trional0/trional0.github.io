Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

WorkBox is a desktop app that runs on the user's own Windows computer. It has no WorkBox server. Everything it stores stays in a database on that computer.

What WorkBox accesses in your Google account

Only if you choose "Connect Google" in WorkBox's settings:

  • Your email address (openid, email), to show which Google account is connected.
  • Calendars that WorkBox creates (calendar.app.created). WorkBox creates one calendar called "WorkBox" and reads, adds, changes and deletes events only in that calendar. It cannot see your other calendars.

How the data is used and stored

  • Events you mark "Sync to Google Calendar" are sent to your "WorkBox" Google calendar. Changes you make to that calendar are copied back into WorkBox on your computer.
  • The Google sign-in token and the synced events are stored only in WorkBox's database on your computer.
  • WorkBox does not send your data anywhere except to Google's Calendar API, does not share or sell it, and does not use it for advertising or for training AI models.

WorkBox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Choose "Disconnect" in WorkBox's settings, or remove WorkBox at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting revokes the token and deletes it from your computer. The "WorkBox" calendar stays in your Google account until you delete it there.
