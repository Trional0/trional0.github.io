WorkBox

WorkBox is a desktop app for a small office. It keeps the office's clients, projects, tasks, files and calendar on the office's own computer.

It can optionally put chosen calendar events into a "WorkBox" calendar in the user's Google account, so they show on their phone, and bring changes made there back into the app.

WorkBox is not sold or offered to the public.
