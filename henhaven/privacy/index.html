Henhaven Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Henhaven ("the game", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. Henhaven is made by Minmax Studios, the trading name of Thomas Nikou, Veria, Greece, who is responsible for your data. You can reach us at MinmaxStudios@proton.me.

The short version: your farm is saved on your phone. You never have to give us your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Everything that leaves your phone is optional: neighbours (an online farm code and a farm name you pick), game stats and crash reports (only if you allow them), a cloud save through Google Play Games (only if you turn it on), rewarded ads you choose to watch, and purchases through Google Play. We never sell your data.

1. Your farm, saved on your device

Your game progress (your farm, animals, coins, Golden Eggs, crates, decorations and settings) is saved in the game's own storage on your device. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this copy is deleted.

Save codes. The Menu has a "Copy save code" button that copies your farm, as text, to your device's clipboard so you can move it yourself. The game does not send this code anywhere.

Your time zone is read so the farm's day starts at 05:00 your time. It stays on your device.

2. Your year of birth

When you start a new farm, the game asks for your year of birth. It is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. If you are under 13, the game hides everything that costs money, does not offer neighbours or usernames, never sends game stats, and only ever requests non-personalized, child-directed ads. The online service only learns whether you are under 13 (yes or no), never the year.

3. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you turn on Cloud save in Settings and sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of your farm is stored in your own Google account's saved games, so you can restore it on another phone. Google stores it under the Google Privacy Policy. We cannot read it. You can delete it from your Google account's Play Games data.

4. Neighbours and online features

Neighbours lets you visit and help other players' farms. It is for players aged 13 and over, and you can turn it off in the Menu. When it is on, the game creates an anonymous online account for your device (no email or password) on our online service, which runs on Supabase in the European Union. It holds:

DataWhy
A random account ID, your farm code and farmer numberSo other players can find and add you

A small picture of your farm (its layout and animals, never your purchases or year of birth)So neighbours can visit your farm

Supabase processes this data for us as our service provider. Mail and gifts are removed after 30 days; farm pictures of players who stop playing after 45 days; other neighbour data after one year without use.

5. Game stats and crash reports (Firebase)

After the age screen the game asks whether you allow game stats and crash reports, and you can change your answer in the Menu at any time. Nothing is sent until you agree. If you agree, Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) receive:

  • Game stats: how you play (for example levels reached, tutorial steps, quests and orders done, which screens you open), a Firebase installation ID, your device model, OS version and approximate country. Players under 13 never send game stats.
  • Crash reports: information about crashes and errors in the game, with the device model and OS version.

We use this only to understand which parts of the game work and to fix problems. We do not use it for advertising, and the game does not send any advertising ID with it. Game stats are kept for 14 months.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Henhaven only offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch a short video for an in-game reward, and the reward is always shown first. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID and app set ID), your approximate location from your IP address, and your interactions with ads, under How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change it at any time in Settings under "Privacy choices". Without consent you may still see non-personalized ads. On any Android device you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's Settings.

7. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The game receives a purchase confirmation, checks it with Google through our online service (section 4), and keeps a record so it can give you what you bought. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

8. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location. We do not sell or share your personal information for others' marketing.

9. Legal basis (EEA and UK)

Game stats, crash reports and personalized ads rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Neighbours and cloud save rely on providing the features you turned on. Checking purchases relies on performing the purchase you asked for and preventing fraud. Non-personalized ads rely on our legitimate interest in offering a free game.

10. Children

Henhaven is meant for players aged 13 and over. Because a younger player may still find it, the game asks for a year of birth (section 2) and turns off purchases, neighbours, usernames, game stats and personalized ads for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. How to delete your Henhaven account and data

To delete your Henhaven (by Minmax Studios) online account and its data:

  1. In the game, open Menu > Neighbours and note your farm code (for example 4K7-Q2M). If you no longer have the game, skip this step.
  2. Email MinmaxStudios@proton.me with the subject "Delete my Henhaven data" and your farm code.
  3. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your online account and everything in section 4: farm code, farmer number, farm name, username, level, last-seen time, farm picture, neighbours, help, gifts, requests, messages, reports and blocks, and our record of your purchases. What is kept: nothing on our online service. Game stats and crash reports are not linked to your account and are removed automatically after 14 months. Google keeps its own records of Play purchases and ads under its policies. The farm saved on your phone stays until you clear the game's data or uninstall it, and a cloud save stays in your Google account until you delete it there.

Your other rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may also have the right to access, correct, or object to the use of your data; email us for any of these. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. If you are in the EEA or UK you may complain to your data protection authority.

12. Security and transfers

All data sent by the game is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our online service is hosted in the EU and protected by access rules so each player can only read what the game shows them. Google may process data outside your country under its own safeguards.

13. Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version of the game is released.
